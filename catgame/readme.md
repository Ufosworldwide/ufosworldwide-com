🐱 Julie's Cattery & Clinic — Player's Guide

    Stardew Valley's heart + Neko Atsume's cat-watching joy + DragonVale's breeding obsession, all in one cozy HTML file.

🎮 How to Start Playing

    Download the julies-cattery.html file.
    Double-click it — it opens in any web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
    No installation. No internet required. No accounts. It just works.
    Your progress auto-saves to your browser. Come back anytime and your cats will be waiting.

🏡 Your Farm
This is your home base. You'll see:

    The Cattery — where all your cats live
    The Clinic — where you heal sick cats
    The Lab — where you track breeds and reputation
    Roaming cats — your cats wander the yard in real time, sleeping, playing, and being cute

Daily Loop

    Check on your cats (feed the hungry ones!)
    Open the Clinic and treat patients
    Breed cats in the Cattery
    Click 🌙 End Day to advance time

🐈 The Cats
Every cat is unique. They have:
Table
Stat	What It Means
❤️ Health	Low health = sick cat. Keep it high with food and medicine.
🍗 Hunger	Drops every day. Feed cats before it hits zero or they lose health.
😊 Happiness	Pet your cats! Happy cats are healthier cats.
⚡ Energy	Used for breeding and playing. Recovers when cats sleep.
Life Stages

    🍼 Kitten (Days 0–13) — Tiny, extra cute, can't breed yet
    🐱 Adult (Days 14–59) — Can breed, work, and be adopted out
    👴 Senior (Day 60+) — Retired from breeding, but wise and lovable

When a Cat Gets Sick
Sick cats show a 😿 bubble in the yard. Take them to the Clinic or open their detail page and click 💊 Treat.
🧬 Breeding — The Heart of the Game
This is where the magic happens. Every cat has a DNA helix showing its genetic makeup.
How to Breed

    Go to the Cattery
    Click a cat card to select it for breeding
    Pick a Queen (female) and a Stud (male)
    Click 💕 Breed ($50)
    Name your kitten!

Genetics 101

    Each cat carries breed percentages from its ancestors
    Kittens inherit colors, patterns, ear shapes, body types, and personalities from both parents
    Crossbreeds are often more valuable than purebreds (the market loves uniqueness!)
    After enough generations, you'll see multi-generation hybrids with wild mixed ancestry

Rare Traits to Watch For

    Colorpoint pattern (Siamese-style masks)
    Folded ears (Scottish Fold gene)
    Tufted ears (Maine Coon / Norwegian Forest)
    Flat faces (Persian)
    Heterochromia (rare eye color mutations — 5% chance!)
    Polydactyly (extra toes — hidden recessive!)

Tips

    Breed cats with different strengths to create well-rounded kittens
    A sick or exhausted cat cannot breed
    Seniors are retired from breeding but can still be therapy cats

🌙 Ending the Day
Click 🌙 End Day on the Farm screen. Here's what happens:

    All cats age by 1 day
    Hunger and energy drop
    Health may drop if cats are neglected
    Random sickness can strike
    Pregnant cats give birth after 3 days
    New clinic patients may arrive
    Breed unlocks are checked

You cannot lose cats permanently — they just get very grumpy if you neglect them for too long. The game is cozy, not punishing.
💾 Saving
Your game saves automatically to your browser's local storage every time you do something meaningful. There is no manual save button.
To transfer your save to another computer or browser, copy this from your browser console:
JavaScript
Copy

localStorage.getItem('julieCatterySave')

Paste it into the new browser's console with:
JavaScript
Copy

localStorage.setItem('julieCatterySave', 'PASTE_HERE')

To reset and start fresh, open your browser console and run:
JavaScript
Copy

localStorage.removeItem('julieCatterySave'); location.reload()

🐾 Tips from the Developer

    Don't rush. This is a game you check in on, not grind through.
    Mixed breeds are trendy. A Domestic × Siamese × Bengal mix often sells for more than a purebred.
    Keep your adults fed and happy — they're your breeding stock.
    Name every kitten. The game feels warmer when every cat has a story.
    Check the yard often. Cats do adorable things when you're not looking.
    The clinic is your money maker early on. Breed for joy, treat for cash.
    Seniors are treasures. Don't adopt them out just because they can't breed. They're the heart of your farm.
