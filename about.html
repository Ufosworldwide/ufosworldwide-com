Mission Statement

UFOs Worldwide is an independent research platform built on a single operating principle: identify gaps, build frameworks, publish findings, and move on. The platform combines structured UAP investigation with rigorous multi-domain science — operating as an independent intelligence architecture rather than a passive observer.

The core methodology is Subtractive Inversion — the four-category elimination framework (Category I: prosaic; Category II: concealed/chronic; Category III: industry artifact; Category IV: genuine anomaly) — developed from decades of field investigation and applied systematically across UAP research, water quality analysis, geophysics, and mathematics.

Presignal Inc. formalizes this research under an independent corporate structure, with an active portfolio spanning earth science, space science, medical research, water intelligence (AquaSignal), cybersecurity (SafeKey), and mathematical frameworks including work touching the Riemann Hypothesis and Cosmic Web topology.

All work is published under CC BY 4.0 license via Zenodo and EarthArXiv. The Anomaly Dashboard integrates live NOAA SWPC Kp-index, USGS earthquake feeds, and NASA DONKI solar events for real-time correlation analysis.
