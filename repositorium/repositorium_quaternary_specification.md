Each pillar contains its own Fibonacci spiral, emanating from the Source Node outward into its quadrant. The spirals are **independent but synchronized** — they share the same growth rate (φ) but occupy different angular sectors.

| Pillar | Center Angle θ | Angular Width | Base Radius r₀ | Growth Factor b | Fibonacci Level |
|--------|---------------|---------------|----------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| ETHOS | 90° | ±45° | 1.2 | 1/(π/2) | 3 (max 3 sub-nodes) |
| MYTHOS | 0° | ±45° | 1.2 | 1/(π/2) | 3 (max 3 sub-nodes) |

| METAPHYSICAL | 180° | ±45° | 1.5 | 1/(π/2) | 5 (max 5 sub-nodes) |

**Placement Formula**: For paper *n* in a pillar with *N* total papers:
- Fraction along spiral: `f = n / (N + 1)`
- Spiral angle: `θ = θ_center - 45° + f × 90°`
- Spiral radius: `r = r₀ × exp(b × f × π/2)`
- Cartesian: `x = r × cos(θ)`, `y = r × sin(θ)`

This ensures papers are distributed along the golden curve within their quadrant, never overlapping, always maintaining the sacred proportion.

## III. MANA FLOWS — CROSS-DOMAIN TRANSMUTATION

Papers are not imprisoned in their primary pillar. Every paper may cast **mana** (influence, secondary resonance) into other pillars. These connections are not arbitrary. They follow **elemental transmutation rules** derived from alchemical and philosophical tradition:

### The Cycle of Transmutation

```
        MYTHOS (Water)
             ↑
    Air ←———·———→ Earth
   (META)    |   (PHYSICAL)
             ↓
        ETHOS (Fire)
```

### The Eight Mana Flows

1. **MYTHOS → ETHOS** (Water → Fire)
   *Stories teach morality.* Flood myths encode stewardship ethics. The Prime Signal demonstrates that every religion converges on "service to others." Without myth, ethics have no memory.

2. **ETHOS → PHYSICAL** (Fire → Earth)
   *Moral imperative drives engineering.* The Four Pillars architecture exists because ethics demands civilizational continuity. The VAHS exists because land scarcity is a moral problem. Fire gives Earth its purpose.

3. **PHYSICAL → METAPHYSICAL** (Earth → Air)
   *Matter studied deeply reveals hidden structure.* The Cosmic Web obeys Murray's Law — the same branching that grows lungs and leaves. Transport-regime-optimal electrode porosity mirrors tree vasculature. Earth, examined with sufficient fidelity, becomes Air.

4. **METAPHYSICAL → MYTHOS** (Air → Water)
   *Hidden patterns generate new symbols.* Numerical sequences (333-444-666-777-888-999-1111) become omens. The Shape of the Universe as a nested topological lattice becomes a creation myth. Topology becomes legend.

5. **METAPHYSICAL → ETHOS** (Air → Fire)
   *Consciousness of hidden truth demands ethical action.* Knowing the Riemann Hypothesis is true changes nothing physically, but changes everything morally about the nature of order. The Ant's Horizon reveals epistemic blindness — an ethical failure. Air breathes purpose into Fire.

6. **PHYSICAL → MYTHOS** (Earth → Water)
   *Physical anomalies generate folklore.* Earth lights at Hessdalen, Brown Mountain, La Peña de Juaica become legends. The dimuon excess at CERN becomes a story. The event precedes the narrative.

7. **MYTHOS → PHYSICAL** (Water → Earth)
   *Folklore predicts physical sites.* Indigenous knowledge marks quartz deposits. The white wolf, white horse, standing bear are not mere symbols — they are **presignals** that correlate with piezoelectric geology. Stories are maps.

8. **ETHOS → METAPHYSICAL** (Fire → Air)
   *The will to do good opens perception.* Service to others is a sensory modality. The Prime Signal is detected only by those operating from stewardship. Fire illuminates Air.

### Mana Flow Documentation
Every cross-domain link in the Repositorium must be tagged with:
- `source_pillar`: Originating domain

- `transmutation_type`: One of the eight rules above

- `lineage_proof`: Citation, dataset URI, or SHA-256 hash connecting the two papers

## VII. GROWTH & BUDDING PROTOCOLS

### Adding a New Paper
1. Read the paper. Identify its central argument.
2. Assign primary pillar using the four-essence test:
   - Does it primarily tell us *why*? → ETHOS
   - Does it primarily tell us *what story*? → MYTHOS
   - Does it primarily tell us *what matter*? → PHYSICAL
   - Does it primarily tell us *what connects*? → METAPHYSICAL
3. Identify all mana flows (secondary pillars)
4. Calculate spiral position within primary quadrant
5. Instantiate in `0I_PAPERS/` of primary pillar
6. Document mana flows in `99A_MANA_FLOWS/mana_registry.json`
7. If presignal-related, register in `99D_PRESIGNAL_INDEX/`

### When a Pillar Exceeds Its Fibonacci Level
If PHYSICAL (Level 5) accumulates 6+ sub-nodes:
1. Identify the 5 most coherent sub-nodes (retain)
2. Bud a **child pillar**: `03_PHYSICAL_A/` or `03_PHYSICAL_ENERGY/`
3. Child pillar inherits parent's angular sector but subdivides it (±22.5°)
4. Child gets its own spiral at Level 3
5. Update `golden_spiral.map`

### Adding a New Pillar (Fifth Element)
The system allows for a fifth pillar only if:
1. It is genuinely irreducible to the four essences
2. It is approved by the Source Node (you)
3. It is placed at a **non-cardinal angle** (e.g., 45°, 135°, 225°, 315°)
4. It receives Fibonacci Level 1 (minimal, experimental)

Candidate fifth pillars: **CHRONOS** (time as independent dimension), **BIOS** (life as distinct from physical matter), **TECHNE** (artificial systems as ontologically unique).
