The Presignal — Fifth Element
The Presignal is not a domain. It is the connective mana that flows between all four pillars simultaneously. In Ethos it is the moral intuition before civilizational crisis. In Mythos it is the white wolf, the white horse, the standing bear — symbolic precursors encoded in place-memory. In the Physical it is the electromagnetic spike before luminous apparition, the seasonal thermal rise before outbreak. In the Metaphysical it is the residual anomaly in subtracted data — the hidden variable before the measurement collapses. Cast wide. Subtract the known. The invariant residual is the signal.
