The Mission

Sentinel turns your phone into a scientific instrument. Your device's sensors are already capturing data you can't perceive — sub-audible infrasound below 20Hz, magnetic field fluctuations, micro-vibrations, and cellular signal anomalies. We collect only the science. Nothing else passes through. Every node adds resolution to a global picture of geophysical and electromagnetic anomalies — the same signals studied at Hessdalen, Brown Mountain, and La Peña de Juaica.


This is open science. All data is CC BY 4.0. You name your node. You own your contribution. The network is run by Presignal Inc., an independent research organization in Niagara Region, Ontario, Canada — ORCID 0009-0004-1363-304X.

We collect only: sensor readings (magnetometer XYZ, infrasound proxy amplitude, accelerometer XYZ, signal strength), your chosen node name, and approximate city-level location estimated from your network (no GPS coordinates, no IP address stored, no personal data).

We never collect: your name, email, phone number, precise location, browsing history, contacts, photos, or any identifying information.

Data use: Scientific anomaly detection research only. Published under CC BY 4.0. Never sold. Never shared with advertisers. Never used for any purpose outside open science.
