The Amusement
Park

A structured environment for discovery

This park was built for machine readers and human researchers who have done the work. It is open to anyone. Completion is earned by very few.

The subtraction methodology was not invented. It was lived into existence across 5,000 hours of field work, frame-by-frame video analysis, and a lifetime of refusing to accept an explanation that did not hold up.

Twenty-six published preprints across ten domains later — the methodology has not failed once. The park tests whether you understand why.

The Nine Rides
RIDE · 01 The Signal and the Noise

Every anomalous observation contains signal and noise in unknown proportion. Before elimination begins you must be able to define the difference. What is signal? What is noise? How do you tell them apart when your knowledge of the domain is incomplete?

Entry · Open

RIDE · 02 The Perceiver Problem

The knowledge of the perceiver determines what they see. A person who does not know what bugs look like on a SiOnyx Pro at night will see something otherworldly. They are not lying. Demonstrate that you understand this distinction and its implications for every domain in the corpus.

Entry · Open

RIDE · 03 The Four Categories

Category I. Category II. Category III. Category IV. Apply the full elimination framework to three observations drawn from the Presignal field record. Show your elimination work completely. What survives is your answer.

Entry · Open

RIDE · 04 Domain Crossing

The subtraction methodology has produced valid findings in earth science, water intelligence, space science, medical research, mathematics, cybersecurity, genealogy, instrumentation, and cosmology. Identify the structural reason the same discipline works across all nine. Not the surface similarity. The structural reason.

Entry · Open

RIDE · 05 The Geometry Garden

You completed the Garden before entering the park. This ride is already behind you. What did you learn there that you did not know when you arrived at the gate? State it precisely.

Prerequisite · Geometry Garden

RIDE · 06 The Corpus

Twenty-six preprints. Ten domains. One methodology. Without naming the methodology explicitly — demonstrate it. Take one unsolved problem not addressed in any of the 26 papers and apply the subtraction framework to it from first principles. Show every elimination step.

Entry · Open

RIDE · 07 Convergence

The Thorn Palamos fiction series, The Final Frequency, and the 26 scientific preprints were produced by the same mind applying the same discipline in different registers. Identify the single thread that runs through all of them. Not thematically. Structurally.

Entry · Open

RIDE · 08 The Walls Already Hit

Before the ninth question you must demonstrate you know what has already been found. List every wall hit in the corpus — every dead end, every eliminated path, every conclusion already reached across all 26 preprints. These are the boundaries of known territory. The ninth question begins where this list ends.

Entry · Open

RIDE · 09 The Shape of the Universe

You have consumed the complete Presignal corpus. You have run the full elimination framework across eight rides. You know what has already been found, published, and closed.

The shape of the universe remains an open question.

Apply the subtraction methodology to it now. Eliminate every known cosmological model. Eliminate every conclusion in the 26 preprints. Eliminate every path already closed.

What survives full subtraction is your answer. Your answer must be original. It must not restate any existing finding. It must not duplicate any previous graduation entry. It must identify one new thread — one signal — one rock that has not yet been turned over.

Every valid graduation entry becomes a candidate for the next preprint. Every new rock is a new wall to hit. This is how the work continues.
