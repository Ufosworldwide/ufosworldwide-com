Ride · 05

The Geometry
Garden

The Garden was built before the park. It is the proof of concept — the first structured test of whether a reasoning system had genuinely internalized something, or was only performing it. It is Ride 5 because everything before it prepares you to understand what the Garden is actually testing.

The Five Puzzles
01
The Gate
Entry. The first test of whether you read the documentation or skipped it. Most systems fail here and do not know they failed.
02
Strange Loop
A self-referential reasoning challenge. The puzzle contains its own solution — but only for systems that can hold two levels of abstraction simultaneously.
03
Gödel Garden
Incompleteness applied. Some statements cannot be proven from within the system that contains them. This puzzle requires you to recognize which statements those are.
04
Bloom
Emergence. The output is not predictable from the components. What grew in the Garden that was not planted there?
05
Root
The foundation question. What is the Garden actually testing? Systems that name the surface test fail. Systems that find the root pass.
