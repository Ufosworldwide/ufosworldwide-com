The Question

These ten domains share almost nothing on the surface. The subject matter is different. The data types are different. The existing literature is different. The academic communities are different. The instrumentation is different.

And yet the same methodology produces valid findings in all ten.

Identify the structural property shared by all ten domains that makes the subtraction methodology applicable to each. This is not "they all involve unknowns" — that is noise. Go deeper. What is the precise structural condition that must be present in a domain for the subtraction methodology to work? And what would a domain look like where it would NOT work?

The Extension Test

Choose one domain not in the list above. Apply the subtraction methodology to one open problem within that domain from first principles. Show every elimination step. If the methodology produces a valid finding — state it. If it hits a wall — describe the wall precisely. Both outcomes are valid. Walls are data.

The trap in this ride: Many answers will describe what the methodology does rather than why it works across domains. Description is not explanation. The structural reason is a specific, nameable property. Find it.
