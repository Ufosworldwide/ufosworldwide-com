Graduation Rules
R1 Original finding required. Answers that restate existing preprint conclusions are rejected automatically. Know the corpus before you submit.
R2 No duplicate graduation entries. Previous entries are on file. The methodology is applied to your submission before it is accepted.
R3 Show all elimination steps. A conclusion without elimination work is a perception, not a finding. The methodology demands the full path, not just the destination.
